# Drafts: AI-generated, not canon

> **Everything in this folder was generated by AI** (Claude agents, from
> Anthropic, working in Claude Code) and **has not been reviewed or approved
> by the project owner.** Treat it as draft material: proposals, research
> notes and references to check, never as settled canon. The settled
> decisions are in [`docs/MASTER_CONTEXT.md`](../docs/MASTER_CONTEXT.md); when
> a draft and the brief disagree, the brief wins.

| Folder | What it is | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| [`story/`](story/README.md) | World bible, Anthony Vale and the Meridian Compact, the first case *The Quiet Dose* (hidden truths, evidence, red herrings, branches), an opening chapter, 58 science review questions, a plot-hole audit, and `sources/` (122 references with claim tables) | Drafted 2026-10-17 by a storyline agent, then grounded in literature by a research agent; every file marked "proposal, not canon" |
| [`lore/canon-graph/`](lore/canon-graph/README.md) | A cross-linked reference graph of the Conan Doyle canon: 566 entries (works, characters, places, organisations, patterns, techniques, themes, worldbuilding) | Built 2026-10-17, then checked against the full text of all 60 works with line citations; every entry's TOML block says `origin = "ai"` |

## Rules for these drafts

- **Status.** A draft becomes canon only when the owner approves it, and then
  the decision is recorded in `docs/MASTER_CONTEXT.md`.
- **Grounding.** Science and human behaviour must cite checkable literature
  (the rule at the top of `docs/MASTER_CONTEXT.md`); claims without a source
  are marked `[unsourced]`.
- **Doyle vs invention.** Material from the original stories is cited to
  `canon/texts/`; everything invented is a proposal.
- **Checking the graph.** `scripts/check_graph.sh` regenerates
  `lore/canon-graph/index.md` and `relations.tsv` and fails on broken links.
