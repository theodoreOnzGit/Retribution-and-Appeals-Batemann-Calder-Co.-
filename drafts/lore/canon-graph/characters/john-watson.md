Army surgeon wounded in Afghanistan, Holmes's friend and the narrator of almost all the canon.

- Struck by a jezail bullet at Maiwand (`text:a-study-in-scarlet.txt#L74`); introduced to Holmes by Stamford (`text:a-study-in-scarlet.txt#L109`) (`text:a-study-in-scarlet.txt#L237`).
- Marries Mary Morstan after *The Sign of the Four* (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4511-L4512`); returns to civil practice (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L71-L72`).
- His wife calls him "James" once (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L4828`).
- He brought only two cases to Holmes's notice: the engineer's thumb and Colonel Warburton's madness (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L7898-L7901`).
