The Metropolitan Police detective force, represented by Lestrade, Gregson, Athelney Jones, Bradstreet and others.

- Holmes's frequent judgment is that the official force is brave but unimaginative (for example Peter Jones, "an absolute imbecile in his profession" but "as brave as a bulldog" (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L1896-L1898`)).
- The force takes public credit for cases Holmes solves (`text:a-study-in-scarlet.txt#L4672-L4673`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4551`).
