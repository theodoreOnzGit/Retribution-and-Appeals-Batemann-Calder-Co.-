A stepfather or father exploits a young woman's income, and acts to stop her marrying.

ADV03, ADV08 and ADV12 are near-variations: each guardian loses money if the woman marries. RET04 varies it with an employer and his partner. Holmes's warnings about isolated households (ADV12 (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L11303-L11316`)) belong here.
