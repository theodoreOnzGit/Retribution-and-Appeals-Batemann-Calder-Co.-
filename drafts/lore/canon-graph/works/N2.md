## Relationships

Captain Morstan and Major Sholto served together at the Andaman Islands (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L628-L630`). Small, Mahomet Singh, Abdullah Khan and Dost Akbar are "the four" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L646`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4336-L4350`). Thaddeus and Bartholomew are Sholto's twin sons (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L892`). By the end Mary Morstan has accepted Watson (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4511-L4512`). Mary is governess to Mrs. Cecil Forrester, a former client of Holmes's, who treats her as a friend (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L376-L378`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1801-L1808`). Morstan and Sholto were "bosom friends" of the 34th Bombay Infantry, and both lost at cards to the prison officials (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L435`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4199-L4222`). Thaddeus's man Williams, a former light-weight champion, brings the party from the Lyceum (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L847-L853`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L904-L906`).

## Actual chronology

1. In the Mutiny, Small, ~~a one-legged soldier~~ **corrected 2026-10-17** no longer a soldier: a crocodile took his right leg in the Ganges and he was invalided out of the army; he was an indigo overseer at Muttra and, after the rising, joined a volunteer corps at Agra "wooden leg and all" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3716-L3725`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3729-L3740`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3789-L3790`). He keeps a postern of the Agra fort with two Sikh troopers, Mahomet Singh and Abdullah Khan, who at knife-point draw him into the plan (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3825-L3841`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3856-L3974`). Achmet, a rajah's servant disguised as a merchant, is led to the gate by Abdullah's foster-brother Dost Akbar; when Achmet runs, Small trips him with his musket and Dost Akbar stabs him (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3943-L3947`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4046-L4062`). They hide the body and the box in the old quarter of the fort, and Small draws four plans signed with "the sign of the four" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4088-L4130`).
2. The four are convicted of Achmet's murder, after the rajah's second servant, set to shadow Achmet, raises the alarm; the Sikhs get penal servitude for life, and Small's death sentence is commuted to the same (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4142-L4163`). Small is sent to Blair Island in the Andamans (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4175-L4176`). Small offers Sholto and Morstan, who are in debt at cards, a share for help to escape (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4194-L4345`).
3. Sholto goes to India, takes the treasure, leaves the army and returns to England (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4354-L4360`).
4. Morstan, on leave, disappears on 3 December 1878 (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L407`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L424`). He had visited Sholto; they quarrel and Morstan dies of a heart attack, striking his head; Sholto and his servant Lal Chowdar, who believes he struck the blow, hide the body, and Sholto keeps Morstan's share as well as his own (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L945-L981`).
5. Small escapes with Tonga, whom he had nursed back to health, killing a Pathan guard with his wooden leg on the way (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4371-L4410`). Some three or four years before the story he reaches England and has a friend inside Pondicherry Lodge (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4419-L4427`). Early in 1882 a letter from India alarms Sholto (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L914`); he dies on 28 April 1882 (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L590`) after seeing his face at the window, before he can tell his sons where the treasure is hidden (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L982`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4429-L4439`). Small leaves the sign of the four on the body.
6. From 4 May 1882, after an advertisement for her address, Mary receives a pearl every year, sent by Thaddeus (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L443-L456`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1036`).
7. Bartholomew finds the treasure in a sealed attic (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1083-L1095`). Thaddeus writes to Mary; Holmes and Watson go with her to Pondicherry Lodge, where Bartholomew lies dead with a thorn in his skin, the room locked, the treasure gone (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1332-L1390`).
8. Jones arrests Thaddeus and the household (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1660`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1898`). Toby follows the creosote to the river (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1881-L2032`). ~~The Irregulars and Holmes trace the *Aurora*~~ **corrected 2026-10-17** The Irregulars search both banks and find nothing (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2521-L2543`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2637-L2640`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2757`). Holmes reasons that Small would hide the launch at a boat-builder's, and, disguised as an old seaman, finds her at the sixteenth yard he tries, Jacobson's (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3150-L3184`).
9. The launch chase down the Thames: Tonga is shot, Small captured (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3244-L3370`).
10. The box is empty: Small has strewn the treasure along the river (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3581-L3653`).

## Apparent explanation

Jones's theory: Thaddeus quarrelled with his brother and killed him for the treasure (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1648-L1671`).

## Culprit and motive

Jonathan Small, for the treasure he and his partners "earned" and were cheated of, and for revenge on Sholto (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4364-L4367`). Tonga killed Bartholomew without Small's intent (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3409`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4458`).

## Evidence and investigative techniques

- Watson's watch: dents, pawnbrokers' scratches, keyhole scratches tell of an older brother who drank (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L237-L336`).
- The poisoned thorn, not English (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1367-L1371`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1589-L1599`).
- The "impossible" reasoning about the sealed room (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1491`).
- Creosote, a small bare foot, a wooden leg (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1461`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1557`).
- Toby the dog (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1752-L1760`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1881`).
- The gazetteer on the Andamans, read with contempt for its subject (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2581-L2584`).
- *Risus sardonicus*: the rigid muscles and fixed grin point to a strychnine-like alkaloid (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1573-L1592`).
- The wooden stump and the rope: round muddy marks on the sill and floor, and blood on the rope where Small skinned his palm (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1431-L1470`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1711-L1720`).
- "When you have eliminated the impossible": not the door, window or chimney, so the roof (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1482-L1499`).
- The Times files: the pearls began within a week of Major Sholto's death (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L586-L604`).
- Questioning Mrs. Smith without seeming to care what she says (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2283-L2376`), and an advertisement worded as a wife's anxiety, so that the fugitives would see nothing in it (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2814-L2824`).

## Red herrings and misdirection

Thaddeus and the household (Holmes suspects the butler Lal Rao was Small's inside man, and Jones ends up holding him (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2095`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4547`)). At Knight's Place two creosote trails cross and Toby follows the wrong one to a barrel in a timber-yard (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2191-L2257`).

## Twists

The treasure is never recovered: Small has strewn it along "five miles or so" of the river (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3656-L3664`). "I get a wife out of it, Jones gets the credit" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4551`). Holmes's old-seaman disguise deceives Watson and Jones in his own rooms (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2903-L2976`).

## Ethical questions

- Small: "Where is the justice that I should give it up...?" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3673-L3683`). The treasure was a rajah's, taken by murder in a war.
- Sholto's betrayal and Morstan's hidden death go unpunished by law.
- Tonga is described in dehumanising, racist terms throughout; any adaptation must not reproduce this.

## Textual notes

The text has "Upper Norword" once, a typo for Norwood (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L589`). Holmes opens with cocaine, a "seven-per-cent. solution" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L61-L64`), and closes reaching for the cocaine-bottle (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4555`).

## Proposal (not canon): nuclear-cyberpunk adaptation

During a colonial-era reactor evacuation, four guards at a decommissioned site steal a cache of separated fissile material or rare isotopes, then are betrayed by two officers who promised them a way out of a penal labour colony. Decades later the betrayer's heirs find the cache, and a former convict comes to collect. The cache, unlike jewels, is dangerous to hold and cannot simply be thrown in a river, which gives the player a radiological-safety problem as well as a moral one: who has a "clean" right to it?
