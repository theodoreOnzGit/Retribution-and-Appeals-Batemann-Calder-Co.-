Consulting detective of 221B Baker Street, central to all 60 works.

## Canon profile

- He meets Watson in a chemical laboratory at Bart's, having just found a reagent for haemoglobin (`text:a-study-in-scarlet.txt#L228-L253`), and they take rooms at 221B Baker Street (`text:a-study-in-scarlet.txt#L386`).
- He calls himself "the last court of appeal" (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L4074`), which bears on the game's title.
- He uses cocaine in a seven-per-cent solution (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L61-L64`); he alternates "between cocaine and ambition" (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L56-L58`).
- He keeps an index of people and cases (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L336-L342`), and commonplace books of cuttings (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L8621-L8623`).
- He admits to having been beaten four times (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L4060-L4061`) and asks to be reminded of "Norbury" (`text:the-memoirs-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L1874-L1875`).
- His knowledge, as Watson first lists it, has deliberate gaps (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L4483-L4494`).
