## Relationships

Captain Morstan and Major Sholto served together at the Andaman Islands (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L628-L630`). Small, Mahomet Singh, Abdullah Khan and Dost Akbar are "the four" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L646`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4336-L4350`). Thaddeus and Bartholomew are Sholto's twin sons (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L892`). By the end Mary Morstan has accepted Watson (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4511-L4512`).

## Actual chronology

1. In the Mutiny, Small, a one-legged soldier (a crocodile took his right leg in the Ganges (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3716-L3719`)), keeps a gate of the Agra fort with two Sikh soldiers, who draw him into murdering the merchant Achmet for a rajah's treasure (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3803-L3950`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4088-L4108`).
2. The four are convicted of Achmet's murder and sent to the Andamans (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4140`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4175-L4176`). Small offers Sholto and Morstan, who are in debt at cards, a share for help to escape (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4194-L4345`).
3. Sholto goes to India, takes the treasure, leaves the army and returns to England (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4354-L4360`).
4. Morstan, on leave, disappears on 3 December 1878 (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L407`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L424`). He had visited Sholto; they quarrel and Morstan dies of a heart attack, striking his head; Sholto hides the body and the treasure (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L945-L979`).
5. Small escapes with Tonga (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4381-L4410`). Early in 1882 a letter from India alarms Sholto (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L914`); he dies on 28 April 1882 (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L590`) after seeing his face at the window, before he can tell his sons where the treasure is hidden (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L982`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4429-L4439`). Small leaves the sign of the four on the body.
6. From 4 May 1882, after an advertisement for her address, Mary receives a pearl every year, sent by Thaddeus (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L443-L456`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1036`).
7. Bartholomew finds the treasure in a sealed attic (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1083-L1095`). Thaddeus writes to Mary; Holmes and Watson go with her to Pondicherry Lodge, where Bartholomew lies dead with a thorn in his skin, the room locked, the treasure gone (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1332-L1390`).
8. Jones arrests Thaddeus and the household (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1660`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1898`). Toby follows the creosote to the river (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1881-L2032`). The Irregulars and Holmes trace the *Aurora* (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2232`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2521-L2543`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3169-L3182`).
9. The launch chase down the Thames: Tonga is shot, Small captured (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3244-L3370`).
10. The box is empty: Small has strewn the treasure along the river (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3581-L3653`).

## Apparent explanation

Jones's theory: Thaddeus quarrelled with his brother and killed him for the treasure (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1648-L1671`).

## Culprit and motive

Jonathan Small, for the treasure he and his partners "earned" and were cheated of, and for revenge on Sholto (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4364-L4367`). Tonga killed Bartholomew without Small's intent (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3409`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4458`).

## Evidence and investigative techniques

- Watson's watch: dents, pawnbrokers' scratches, keyhole scratches tell of an older brother who drank (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L237-L336`).
- The poisoned thorn, not English (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1367-L1371`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1589-L1599`).
- The "impossible" reasoning about the sealed room (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1491`).
- Creosote, a small bare foot, a wooden leg (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1461`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1557`).
- Toby the dog (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1752-L1760`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L1881`).
- The gazetteer on the Andamans, read with contempt for its subject (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2581-L2584`).

## Red herrings and misdirection

Thaddeus and the household (Holmes suspects the butler Lal Rao was Small's inside man, and Jones ends up holding him (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2095`) (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4547`)). At Knight's Place two creosote trails cross and Toby follows the wrong one to a barrel in a timber-yard (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L2191-L2257`).

## Twists

The treasure is never recovered. "I get a wife out of it, Jones gets the credit" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4551`).

## Ethical questions

- Small: "Where is the justice that I should give it up...?" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L3673-L3683`). The treasure was a rajah's, taken by murder in a war.
- Sholto's betrayal and Morstan's hidden death go unpunished by law.
- Tonga is described in dehumanising, racist terms throughout; any adaptation must not reproduce this.

## Textual notes

The text has "Upper Norword" once, a typo for Norwood (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L589`). Holmes opens with cocaine, a "seven-per-cent. solution" (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L61-L64`), and closes reaching for the cocaine-bottle (`text:the-sign-of-the-four.txt#L4555`).

## Proposal (not canon): nuclear-cyberpunk adaptation

During a colonial-era reactor evacuation, four guards at a decommissioned site steal a cache of separated fissile material or rare isotopes, then are betrayed by two officers who promised them a way out of a penal labour colony. Decades later the betrayer's heirs find the cache, and a former convict comes to collect. The cache, unlike jewels, is dangerous to hold and cannot simply be thrown in a river, which gives the player a radiological-safety problem as well as a moral one: who has a "clean" right to it?
