The social world the stories take for granted: rank, money, servants, marriage, and the city against the country.

## What the canon shows (checked)

- **Rank and money.** Old titles borrow against their heirlooms (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L9875-L9882`) and marry new American money (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L8932-L8951`); a royal duke's grandson robs banks (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L1873-L1881`); a duke buys silence (`text:the-return-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L4976-L4982`).
- **Women's money.** A daughter's or ward's small income is controlled by a stepfather or father, and her marriage would remove it: ADV03 (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L2321-L2323`), ADV08 (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L6916-L6919`), ADV12 (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L11870-L11877`). A governess is glad of £100 a year (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L11124`).
- **Servants** are witnesses, accomplices and protectors: Bannister (`text:the-return-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L8570-L8577`), Theresa Wright (`text:the-return-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L10745`), Mrs. Toller (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L11862-L11865`), Barrymore (`text:the-hound-of-the-baskervilles.txt#L3786-L3799`).
- **City and country.** Holmes thinks the lonely countryside more dangerous than the worst London alley, because public opinion and the police are far away (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L11286-L11316`).
- **Poverty as an occupation.** A beggar out-earns a reporter (`text:the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L5742-L5762`).

## Historical context (recall, not checked)

Married women's property in England was reformed by the Married Women's Property Acts (1870, 1882), which is relevant to why a woman's income mattered so much to the men around her. Divorce was difficult and expensive throughout the period (compare Sterndale, who "could not divorce" (`text:his-last-bow-06-the-devils-foot.txt#L934`)). These are general historical statements made from recall and should be checked before use.

## Proposal (not canon)

The 2189 setting (§3) maps these directly: privileged access to AI and healthcare in place of titles and land; guardianship of compensation awards or reactor-share incomes in place of a ward's annuity; maintenance staff as the modern servants who see everything.
