# The Quiet Dose: evidence matrix (Option A)

> **Status: proposal, not canon.** Written for the recommended option in
> `hidden-truth.md` (culprit Julian Marsh). Numbers marked *(placeholder)*
> are illustrative and must be set or approved by the scientific director;
> each links to a question in `story/science-review-questions.md` (SQ-nn).
> Days are relative to Elias's death (D0); play begins at D+5.

## How to read an entry

- **Where / how:** location in the world and what the player must do to get
  it (the gate).
- **Proves:** what a careful reasoner may conclude from it alone.
- **Does NOT prove:** the tempting conclusion it does not support.
- **Wrong theory it feeds:** which mistaken theory (T1 to T8 below) it seems
  to support, if any.
- **Insight:** the Scientific Insight subject it teaches or requires (brief
  §10).

## Group 1: the official story and why it needs an acute event

**E01. Coroner's finding and HALCYON's exposure assessment**
- *Where / how:* Mara's copy, given at the first meeting (D+5).
- *Proves:* the official reasoning: an acute whole-body external dose of
  3 to 4 Gy *(placeholder, SQ-14)* estimated from blood counts, attributed to
  the Vault 3 entry "plus cumulative occupational exposure".
- *Does NOT prove:* that Vault 3 delivered such a dose; nobody checked.
- *Wrong theory:* T1.
- *Insight:* Radiation dosimetry (gray versus sievert).

**E02. Elias's lifetime occupational dose record**
- *Where / how:* Mara, as next of kin, requests it from B&C (granted D+6).
- *Proves:* a lifetime external effective dose of about 180 mSv over 43
  years *(placeholder, SQ-08)*, every year within the limits; routine
  bioassay eight months earlier was negative.
- *Does NOT prove:* that Elias had no intake on D−19; routine bioassay was
  not scheduled again for four months.
- *Wrong theory:* none directly; it undermines the "cumulative" half of T1,
  because a dose spread over decades does not cause acute marrow failure
  (SQ-09).
- *Insight:* Radiation dosimetry; stochastic versus deterministic effects.
- *Literature check (2026-10-17):* about 4.2 mSv a year is above present-day
  averages for nuclear plant workers (about 1 mSv) `[src: unscear2008,
  japan-npp-dose2008]`; plausible only if early HTGR years were much higher.
  The number stays the director's (SQ-08; `sources/science-claims.md` LC-5).

**E03. The clinical course**
- *Where / how:* Barts chart summary, released to Mara (D+6). Mara's own
  memory of him not eating from about D−16.
- *Proves:* an acute, high-dose pattern: early symptoms, a short apparent
  improvement, then marrow failure and decline over about three weeks
  (SQ-11, SQ-13). The exposure or intake happened roughly three weeks before
  death.
- *Does NOT prove:* internal versus external, or the exact date; D−23
  (vault) and D−19 (dinner) both fit the clinical timing.
- *Wrong theory:* T1 (the timing seems to fit the vault).
- *Insight:* Radiation dosimetry (acute effects).

## Group 2: the vault cannot have done it (the *Bruce-Partington* group)

**E04. Vault 3 access log and electronic dosimeter dock log**
- *Where / how:* B&C disclosure to Mara (D+7); also in Hale's notification.
- *Proves:* Elias's badge entered Vault 3 at 23:10 and left at 23:32 on D−23;
  his electronic dosimeter stayed in its charging dock all night.
- *Does NOT prove:* what dose he received; that he was careless rather than
  deliberate.
- *Wrong theory:* T1.
- *Insight:* What a dosimeter records.

**E05. Vault 3 radiological survey map**
- *Where / how:* B&C's quarterly survey, posted at the vault entrance and
  filed with the Directorate. Hale hands it over if the player is courteous
  and does not accuse him (Respect); otherwise by Appeal Bench order.
- *Proves:* with the maximum aisle and contact dose rates on the map
  *(placeholder, SQ-45)*, **dose rate × time** `[src: canteach-isl]` for 22 minutes gives at most a
  few millisieverts, even standing in contact with the hottest package the
  whole time. The vault cannot have delivered 3 to 4 Gy; it falls short by a
  factor of about a thousand.
- *Does NOT prove:* where the real dose came from.
- *Wrong theory:* none; it refutes T1. It is the case's first turning point.
- *Insight:* Radiation dosimetry (dose rate, time, distance; Gy versus Sv).

**E06. Exit contamination monitor record, D−23, 23:33**
- *Where / how:* B&C site logs (same disclosure as E04).
- *Proves:* no detectable surface contamination on Elias when he left.
- *Does NOT prove:* that he had no internal contamination; a surface monitor
  is not designed to answer that (SQ-17).
- *Wrong theory:* none; it weakens the idea that the vault contaminated him.
- *Insight:* Measurement and uncertainty (what an instrument is for).

**E07. Voss's passive dosimeter**
- *Where / how:* in Voss's loan register (serial number) and her readout.
  She produces it only once she trusts the player (Trust with Voss ≥ a
  threshold, or after the player shows E05 and promises to protect her
  licence where honestly possible).
- *Proves:* an external dose of about 0.3 mSv *(placeholder, SQ-46)* during
  the period it was worn, consistent with E05.
- *Does NOT prove:* that Elias wore it on his body throughout (B&C's lawyers
  will say he left it outside the door). E08's timestamps answer that.
- *Wrong theory:* T4 (Voss was complicit in a breach).
- *Insight:* What a dosimeter records; calibration.

**E08. Voss's portable spectrometer data**
- *Where / how:* Voss's lab, with its calibration certificate traceable to the
  Metrology Institute (same gate as E07).
- *Proves:* six measurements at six package positions, timestamped
  23:12 to 23:30 on D−23, so Elias was in the vault and moving among the
  packages, which places the passive dosimeter there too. The measured
  activities are far above the declared inventories *(placeholder, SQ-43)*.
- *Does NOT prove:* anything about the cause of death. It proves why Elias
  went in.
- *Wrong theory:* T2 (B&C has a liability motive); T3 (Hale "reconciled" the
  same discrepancies, SQ-44).
- *Insight:* Spectroscopy; calibration and instrument integrity.

**E09. Biodosimetry result**
- *Where / how:* Barts chart (D+6), with HALCYON's note "inconsistent with
  clinical picture; probable assay artefact".
- *Proves:* the chromosome-based estimate is an equivalent acute whole-body
  external dose well under 1 Gy, inconsistent with the uniform 3 to 4 Gy
  external dose HALCYON assumed.
- *Does NOT prove:* an internal intake; interpreting this assay for internal,
  non-uniform irradiation is difficult (SQ-20;
  `[src: iaea-biodos2011, ainsbury2023]`).
- *Wrong theory:* T1 (if the player accepts "artefact").
- *Insight:* Measurement and uncertainty; radiation dosimetry.

## Group 3: the dose was inside him (the *Dying Detective* group)

**E10. Whole-body count record and calibration certificate**
- *Where / how:* Barts chart (D+6); certificate from Barts' medical physics
  department.
- *Proves:* the counter was in calibration and found no significant internal
  contamination **of the kind it is built to detect**.
- *Does NOT prove:* that there was no internal contamination (SQ-18, SQ-19;
  `[src: nrc-wbc]`).
  HALCYON treated it as if it did.
- *Wrong theory:* T1.
- *Insight:* Calibration and instrument integrity ("the instrument was right
  and answered a different question").

**E11. HALCYON's decision log**
- *Where / how:* Mara can read AI decision logs (her profession); she needs a
  Bench disclosure order for B&C's system, or the player persuades a B&C
  compliance officer. The player and Mara read it together.
- *Proves:* HALCYON started from its blood-count dose estimate, then searched
  the employer record for an occupational event large enough to supply it,
  found the Vault 3 note and stopped. It down-weighted the biodosimetry under
  a configuration setting, "employer exposure record priority: high".
- *Does NOT prove:* deliberate manipulation. The setting is B&C's standard
  configuration for every worker and predates the case by years. (Automation
  bias of this kind is documented `[src: parasuraman2010, goddard2012]`.)
- *Wrong theory:* T2 (the setting looks sinister).
- *Insight:* Measurement and uncertainty (reasoning backwards from a target).

**E12. Medical proxy designation and the D−11 decision**
- *Where / how:* Barts chart, the consent section, released only to Mara;
  revealed in a scene in which Mara learns her father's proxy was still Uncle
  Jules. Gated until after E13, so it is not the first thing the player sees.
- *Proves:* Julian, as proxy, declined further investigations including the
  extended bioassay Dr Amari suggested and HALCYON rated "low yield".
- *Does NOT prove:* bad intent. Comfort-focused care for a dying man is a
  common and compassionate choice, and the AI advised the test was of little
  value.
- *Wrong theory:* none; it supports the sympathetic reading of Julian as
  strongly as the guilty one.
- *Insight:* none (character evidence).

**E13. The biobank samples and Voss's radiochemistry**
- *Where / how:* Barts' 30-day diagnostic biobank (SQ-27). The player must
  get a **preservation order** from the Appeal Bench before D+18 to keep the
  earliest samples (D−12 onward); later samples survive to D+29. Chain-of-
  custody forms must be completed at transfer (SQ-25). Voss analyses them.
  The player learns the biobank exists from Voss, or, if the player has not
  yet gone to her, from Dr Amari when interviewed at Barts.
- *Proves:* the samples contain the contaminant, a radionuclide internally
  deposited in Elias's body, at levels consistent with the clinical course
  (SQ-12, SQ-23).
- *Does NOT prove:* when, where, by whom, or that it was not occupational.
- *Wrong theory:* T4 ("how did she know to look so fast?"); T6.
- *Insight:* Radioactive decay; isotope analysis; radiation dosimetry
  (internal dose).

**E14. Replication by a second laboratory**
- *Where / how:* split samples sent to the Metrology Institute's reference
  laboratory (the player must think of it, or be prompted by the Insight
  "independent replication"). Results in about four game days.
- *Proves:* E13 is not an artefact of Voss's laboratory; removes the
  conflict-of-interest objection (she was his partner) `[src: iso17025]`.
- *Does NOT prove:* anything beyond E13.
- *Wrong theory:* none; it disarms T4's evidential attack.
- *Insight:* Measurement and uncertainty (replication, inter-laboratory
  comparison).

**E15. Intake-date estimate**
- *Where / how:* Voss and the reference laboratory fit the sample series
  with a biokinetic model and decay correction (SQ-24, SQ-28;
  `[src: ideas2006, icrp130]`).
- *Proves:* the intake most likely happened between D−21 and D−17, most
  likely D−19, **after** the vault entry (D−23). Timing excludes the vault a
  second, independent way.
- *Does NOT prove:* which event in the window.
- *Wrong theory:* none.
- *Insight:* Radioactive decay; measurement and uncertainty.

**E16. Provenance fingerprint**
- *Where / how:* Voss compares the contaminant's isotopic and impurity
  fingerprint with (a) the Vault 3 material she measured in E08 and (b) the
  Metrology Institute's reference library of radiocatalysis feedstock classes
  (SQ-29, SQ-30; `[src: mayer2005, iaea-nss2g]`).
- *Proves:* the contaminant matches a class of radiocatalysis feedstock and
  **does not** match Erith Reach legacy material. **The death was not
  occupational at Erith Reach.**
- *Does NOT prove:* which lot, or who. The class was supplied to several
  yards over several years.
- *Wrong theory:* T5 (Crane has worked in Dagenham radiocatalysis yards since
  he was laid off). It also kills T1 outright.
- *Insight:* Isotope analysis; material provenance.

## Group 4: where and who (the *Silver Blaze* group)

**E17. Elias's movements in the intake window**
- *Where / how:* transit logs (Mara), Elias's diary (Mara has it), Voss's
  account, union minutes.
- *Proves:* three events fall in the window: supper at Voss's lab (D−20, a
  takeaway they shared), the monthly dinner at Julian's (D−19), the union
  meeting (D−17, buffet for forty).
- *Does NOT prove:* which event.
- *Wrong theory:* T4 and T5 both gain a place and a time.
- *Insight:* none.
- *The deduction it enables (with E26):* of the three, **only at the dinner did
  one person control everything Elias ate and drank.** At the lab, the food
  came from a shop and both ate it; at the union meeting, forty people shared
  a buffet. This is the *Silver Blaze* curry reasoning
  (text:the-memoirs-of-sherlock-holmes.txt#L993-L1008), and it narrows
  suspicion without describing any act.

**E18. Contamination surveys**
- *Where / how:* the Radiological Incidents Unit (Okafor) surveys at the
  player's request once E13 and E16 are in hand: Elias's home, car and
  office, Voss's lab, the union hall. **Julian's flat needs an Appeal Bench
  order**, granted only if the player presents E15, E16, E17, E19 and E20
  together.
- *Proves:* no contaminant traces in Elias's car or office, at Voss's lab
  (only legacy-signature traces on the spectrometer case, from Vault 3) or at
  the union hall. Traces in Elias's bathroom at home only from after D−19,
  consistent with excretion after an intake (SQ-32). **Faint traces with the
  matching fingerprint in Julian's dining room and kitchen**, despite
  cleaning (SQ-31), at no hazard to anyone.
- *Does NOT prove:* that Julian put them there. His defence: Elias brought
  the contamination in with him. Answer: Elias's car, office and the places
  he went before D−19 are clean; his home has traces only after D−19.
- *Wrong theory:* the clean result at Voss's lab and the union hall disposes
  of T4 and T5.
- *Insight:* Material provenance; measurement and uncertainty (detection
  limits, background).

**E19. Directorate source register extract**
- *Where / how:* Mara's public-records request (she knows exactly how to
  word it); or the player's own after the Insight "material provenance".
- *Proves:* one lot of the matching feedstock class, from the Dagenham yard
  that went bankrupt in 2187, is recorded as "returned to supplier; verified
  J. Marsh".
- *Does NOT prove:* that the lot went missing, or that Julian took it.
- *Wrong theory:* T5 (Crane worked at that very yard in its last year).
- *Insight:* Material provenance.

**E20. The supplier's receiving records**
- *Where / how:* the supplier's depot in Tilbury. Route depends on earlier
  choices: Crane's union contacts at the depot (if the player did not accuse
  Crane), or B&C procurement (if the player kept Hale's goodwill), or a Bench
  order (slow).
- *Proves:* no receipt of that lot. The lot is unaccounted for.
- *Does NOT prove:* that Julian has or had it. A clerical failure is possible;
  the supplier has its own reasons to hide a loss.
- *Wrong theory:* none.
- *Insight:* Material provenance (chain of custody of materials).

**E21. The Directorate notification marked "reviewed: no action"**

- *Proves:* Julian knew of the Vault 3 entry on D−21, two days before the

- *Does NOT prove:* intent. Read kindly, it is a friend protecting a friend.
- *Wrong theory:* none; it is the case's *Silver Blaze* dog, but only once

**E22. The deep-clean booking**

**E23. Nurse Anand's testimony**

- *Proves:* Julian asked three times whether "the counter found anything,

**E24. Coroner's clerk's note of Julian's D+1 call**

- *Proves:* Julian asked for the fast-track "for the family's sake".

**E25. Elias's personal vault: the statement and the diary**
- *Where / how:* his personal data vault. The passphrase comes from a memory

- *Proves:* the D−26 statement about the 2158 survey naming Elias and Julian

- *Does NOT prove:* that Julian acted on it.

**E26. What the player saw in the opening chapter** (journal entries)
- *Where / how:* the player's own journal, written during the opening chapter.
- *Proves (as setup):* (a) Julian insisted on formally reporting a dock
  worker's minor dosimeter-storage breach although Elias asked for leniency,
  and said, "I report everything. The day I stop is the day the job stops."
  (b) "Jules cooks; I bring the wine" (Elias, inviting the player to a future
  dinner). (c) Julian grumbled about "chasing orphan-source paperwork from
  every bankrupt yard in Dagenham". (d) Mara: Uncle Jules "signed my school
  forms when Mum was ill". (e) Elias made the player wear a visitor dosimeter
  on the dock and said he had never walked into a controlled area without
  one.
- *Does NOT prove:* anything on its own; these are the facts the later
  evidence is measured against.
- *Insight:* none.

**E27. The 2158 survey: original and rewritten**
- *Where / how:* the Directorate archive at Woolwich Reach. The rewritten
  version is in the public file; the original survey sheets survive in Elias's
  personal vault (he kept copies for 31 years) and in a box in the archive's
  uncatalogued legacy store (found with the archivist's help).
- *Proves:* the falsification; that Ward and Marsh countersigned the
  rewritten version; that E08's package activities are consistent with the
  original survey and not the rewritten one (SQ-37).
- *Does NOT prove:* who ordered it, or any individual health harm (SQ-39).
- *Wrong theory:* T7 (if Vale is mentioned in the 2158 papers; whether he is
  stays open).
- *Insight:* Radioactive decay (reconstructing a 31-year-old activity).

**E28. Hale's calibration secret (red herring)**
- *Where / how:* Hale's dosimetry service records; a dosimetry technician
  who resents him.
- *Proves:* the site's dosimeter reader under-read by about 12 % for 18
  months *(placeholder, SQ-41)*; Hale found it, corrected it going forward
  and did not report the back-correction.
- *Does NOT prove:* anything about Elias's death. Twelve per cent of a small
  dose is a small dose; the discrepancy in this case is a factor of about a
  thousand.
- *Wrong theory:* T3 (records were "doctored").
- *Insight:* Calibration and instrument integrity (size of an error versus
  size of a discrepancy).
- *Literature check (2026-10-17):* the ICRP accepts an overall accuracy of a
  factor of 1.5 either way near the dose limits, and real services'
  uncertainties near 20 mSv were 14–40 % `[src: icrp75-via-apac2020]`, so a
  12 % bias is inside tolerance and might pass an intercomparison
  `[src: eurados-ic]`. Proposed fix (owner's choice): make the error clearly
  out of tolerance, or keep 12 % and make the secret "knew of a systematic
  bias and chose not to correct past records", which accredited labs must act
  on `[src: iso17025]`. Not changed here (SQ-41; `sources/science-claims.md`
  LC-1).

**E29. Julian's interview contradictions**
- *Where / how:* any conversation with Julian after E25 is in hand.
- *Proves:* he says he and Elias "hadn't talked shop in months"; E25 shows
  they discussed 2158 on D−25. He calls the dinner "ordinary"; E22 says
  otherwise. On D+4 he offered to "sort out Dad's papers" for Mara, which she
  had not yet done (Mara mentions it, puzzled, if asked about him).
- *Does NOT prove:* murder. It proves he is hiding the 2158 conversation,
  which he has every reason to hide even if innocent of the killing.
- *Insight:* none.

## The solution chain (fair-play check)

**Question 1: Was the official story true?** No.
- E01 + E02 + E03: the official story needs an acute event; cumulative dose
  cannot explain acute marrow failure (SQ-09).
- E04 + E05 + E07 + E08 + E09: the only acute event offered, Vault 3, cannot
  supply the dose (dose rate × time, a passive dosimeter that was there, a
  biodosimetry result that disagrees).
- **All of these are available by about D+10 without any order except
  possibly E05.**

**Question 2: Then what happened to him?** An internal intake.
- E10 explains why the counter missed it (instrument limitation).
- E13 + E14: the contaminant is in his samples, confirmed independently.
- **Requires the preservation order before D+18 for the best data; later
  samples suffice for a wider window (see `branches.md`).**

**Question 3: Was it occupational?** No.
- E15: intake after the vault entry.
- E16: material from outside Erith Reach.

**Question 4: Where?**
- E17 + E26(b): three events; only the dinner had a single controller.
- E18 (surveys of the other two places): clean.

**Question 5: Who, and how could they have had the material?**
- E19 + E20 + E26(c): a lot of the matching class, verified as returned by
  Julian, never received.
- E18 (Julian's flat) + E22: traces at the dinner's location, and a cleaning
  the next morning.

**Question 6: Why?**
- E25 + E27: the 2158 falsification and the 14th deadline.
- E21 + E26(a): the man who reports everything did not report this.
- E12 + E23 + E24: he blocked the test, watched the counter, hurried the
  finding.

**Can the player name Julian before the game reveals it?** Yes. After
Questions 1 to 4, Julian is one of two hosts in the window; E26(b) and the
surveys make him the only single-controller host. Question 5 gives means
before the Bench order is sought. E25 and E27 (motive) can come before or
after; a player who finds motive first will suspect Julian earlier, which is
fair.

**Does any step depend on a clue the player cannot get?** No. Each gated
clue has at least two routes (see "Where / how"), and none requires a single
dialogue choice made correctly.

**Does any step depend on science the player has not been taught?** Each
step's Insight is unlockable before it is needed: dose rate × time on the
dock in the opening chapter; "what a dosimeter records" in the opening
chapter; what a counter can see at Voss's lab on first visit; decay and
provenance from Voss before E16.

**Does the physics alone prove murder?** No. Questions 1 to 3 establish a
non-occupational internal intake of material from outside Erith Reach,
which is equally consistent with accident (T8) and suicide (T6). Murder
needs Questions 4 to 6, which rest on records, testimony and character, and
intent remains for a court (brief §4: measurements do not establish intent).

## What the player may legitimately still be unsure about at the end

- How Julian came to have the material, and how the intake happened. The
  game never shows it (safety boundary, `hidden-truth.md`).
- Whether Julian intended death or something less. Only his confession, if
  the player obtains it, speaks to that, and a court may not believe it.
- Who ordered the 2158 falsification.
